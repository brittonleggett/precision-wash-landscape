# Keyword & search-intent map

One page, one job. Every page below targets a single primary intent; nothing is
duplicated across pages, and no page is stuffed with terms it can't honestly answer.

**Scope discipline:** this business really does four things
(pressure/soft washing, flowerbed work, junk removal, window washing) in two parishes
(Ouachita, Lincoln) across three named towns (Monroe, West Monroe, Ruston). Every
keyword below stays inside that box. Terms like "lawn care Monroe" or
"landscape design" are deliberately **not** targeted — see *Deliberately not targeted*
at the bottom.

## Why there are no `/areas/[city]/` pages

The brief flagged mass-generated city pages as low quality, and that's the right call
here. Three near-identical pages that differ only by swapping "Monroe" for "Ruston" are
exactly the doorway-page pattern Google demotes, and they'd dilute the one strong
geographic page rather than add to it.

**A city page becomes worth building when it can carry something the service-area page
can't** — specifically:

- Before/after photos from jobs actually done in that town, captioned as such
- A named neighbourhood or two where you genuinely work
- Reviews from customers in that city
- A property-type angle that's real for that town and not the others

Ruston is the most likely first candidate, because the college-town rental-turnover
angle is genuinely different from Monroe's older shaded neighbourhoods — and that
difference is already noted on the service-area page. **Build `/service-area/ruston/`
only once there are 3+ Ruston job photos and at least one Ruston review to put on it.**

Until then: one honest service-area page beats three thin ones.

|---|---|
| lawn care / lawn mowing Monroe | No evidence the business mows. Ranking for it generates calls they'd have to turn down. |
| landscape design / hardscaping | Not a listed service. |
| commercial pressure washing *(as a primary)* | Mentioned honestly on-page as something they'll take, but there's no commercial portfolio to justify a dedicated page yet. |
| gutter cleaning *(as its own page)* | Real, but covered as a surface on the pressure washing page. Not enough distinct content for a page of its own. If Search Console later shows gutter queries pulling impressions, revisit. |

| Competitor brand names | Black-hat. Not doing it. |
