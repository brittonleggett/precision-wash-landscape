# Local backlink & citation plan

The goal is a small number of **real** links from places that genuinely have something
to do with this business or this part of Louisiana. Nothing bought, nothing
manufactured, nothing from a link network.

For a local service business, links matter less than the Google Business Profile and
review count — but **citations** (any mention of your name, service area and phone,
linked or not) are what convince Google the business is real and consistently located.
That's the main job here.

**Do these in order.** The first tier is free, takes an afternoon, and does most of the
work.

---

## Tier 1 — Core citations (do these first, ~2 hours total)

Free, high-trust, and the ones Google actually cross-references.

| Platform | What it is | Effort | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | The big one. Not optional. | 30 min | [setup doc](GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md) |
| **Bing Places** | Bing's equivalent; can import from Google | 10 min | <https://www.bingplaces.com> |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Apple Maps — every iPhone that asks Siri for a pressure washer | 20 min | <https://businessconnect.apple.com> |
| **Facebook page** | Already exists. Make sure the NAP and website link are exact. | 10 min | [existing page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61590393561873) |
| **Nextdoor Business** | Genuinely strong for home services — neighbours ask for recommendations constantly | 20 min | <https://business.nextdoor.com> |
| **Yelp** | Claim it. A listing may already exist without your knowledge. | 15 min | <https://biz.yelp.com> |

**Nextdoor deserves emphasis.** For pressure washing and yard work in residential
neighbourhoods, "does anyone know a good pressure washer?" posts on Nextdoor generate
real calls, and it's free. Worth more here than most directory listings.

Every one of these must use the exact NAP from
[GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md](GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md) — same name spelling, same
phone format, same website URL. Inconsistency is the thing that dilutes the benefit.

## Tier 2 — Local organisations (real relationships, real links)

These take longer and involve talking to people, which is exactly why they're worth
more than any directory.

### Monroe Chamber of Commerce
<https://www.monroe.org> — membership includes a directory listing with a real link
from a genuinely local, genuinely trusted domain. There's a fee; for a local service
business it's usually justified by the listing plus the networking. Ruston-Lincoln
Chamber (<https://rustonlincoln.org>) is the equivalent if Ruston becomes a bigger share
of the work.

### Better Business Bureau
BBB accreditation costs money and the SEO value is modest, but the **trust signal** for
a home-services business letting strangers onto their property is real. Worth
considering once there's steady revenue, not now.

### Suppliers and manufacturers
Where do you buy the pressure washer, chemicals, pine straw and mulch? Many equipment
manufacturers and regional suppliers run a "find a pro" or "where to buy / who uses us"
page. A link from a supplier is topically relevant and completely legitimate. **Just
ask** — a lot of these are handed out for free to actual customers.

### Local sponsorships
Youth sports teams, school fundraisers, church events, 4-H, Little League. Sponsorship
pages routinely link to sponsors, and a link from a school or community organisation in
Ouachita Parish is exactly the kind of local signal that's hard to fake. Modest cost,
real community presence, and it puts the business name in front of the exact
demographic that hires pressure washers.

**This is probably the highest-value item in Tier 2** — it's genuine community
involvement that happens to produce a link.

### Real partnerships
Businesses that serve the same customers but don't compete:
- Roofers (they see algae-streaked roofs constantly)
- Realtors and property managers — **especially** in Ruston, given the rental turnover
  market already noted on the service-area page
- Lawn mowing services (they mow, you do beds — genuinely complementary)
- Home inspectors
- Painters (surfaces need washing before painting)

A referral relationship with two or three of these is worth more in actual revenue than
every directory on this page combined. A link from their site is a bonus.

## Tier 3 — Local media (opportunistic)

Don't chase these; take them if they appear.

- **The News-Star** (Monroe) and **Ruston Daily Leader** — local papers occasionally
  run new-business or small-business features. Free to pitch.
- **KNOE / KARD / KTVE** — local TV sometimes runs "local business" or seasonal
  segments (storm cleanup is a natural hook after severe weather).
- **Storm angle.** After a significant storm, local outlets look for cleanup contractors
  to quote. If the business is doing limb removal after a weather event, that's a
  legitimate, timely pitch — and it lands exactly when people are searching for it.

---

## Tier 4 — Industry directories (low priority, low effort)

Worth an hour, total. Don't over-invest.

- **Angi / HomeAdvisor** — free listing available; the paid lead product is expensive
  and the lead quality is widely complained about. **List, don't buy leads.**
- **Thumbtack** — same advice.
- **Houzz** — more design-oriented; marginal for pressure washing.
- **Pressure Washing Resource Association** or similar trade bodies — if the business
  ever joins one, member directories are relevant links.

---

## Things to actively avoid

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy backlinks / "500 links for $50" | Fastest route to a manual penalty. |
| Paid guest posts on unrelated blogs | Google treats these as link schemes. |
| Mass directory submission services | Hundreds of junk citations, often with **inconsistent NAP**, which actively hurts local ranking. |
| Blog comment / forum signature links | Spam. Ignored at best. |
| Private blog networks (PBNs) | Manual action risk. |
| Reciprocal link swaps at scale | "Link to me and I'll link to you" across dozens of sites is a recognised scheme. A genuine partnership with one local roofer is not. |
| Fake location pages to attract links | Covered in the keyword map — not doing it. |

## Realistic expectations

| Timeframe | What's achievable |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | All of Tier 1 done — 6 solid citations |
| Month 3 | Chamber listing, 2–3 supplier or partner links |
| Month 6 | A sponsorship link, a handful of natural mentions |
| Year 1 | 10–20 real local citations |

That's a good position for a local service business. **Twenty real local citations
outperform two hundred junk ones**, and nothing here needs a budget beyond a Chamber
membership and a team sponsorship.
