# Content roadmap

## The rule: don't publish a blog

A weekly blog on a 7-page pressure-washing site is wasted effort. Nobody reads it, it
dilutes the pages that matter, and generic AI-written filler actively damages a site's
quality signals.

**What follows is seven pages, prioritised, to be built over roughly a year — not seven
pages a month.** Several should not be built at all until a specific condition is met.
Those conditions are listed, and they're real gates, not suggestions.

**The best source of future topics is not this list.** It's Search Console →
Performance → Queries, three months from now. When real people are typing real
questions and you can see them, build for those instead of guessing. Revisit this
roadmap then and expect to change it.

## Priority 1 — Build after the site is live and indexed

### 1. How much does pressure washing cost in Monroe, LA?

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search intent** | Commercial investigation — comparing options, pre-purchase |
| **Target page** | New: `/pressure-washing-cost/` |
| **Priority** | **High** |
| **Why it matters** | Price is the single most-searched question in this trade, and the one every customer has before calling. Right now the site says "call for a free estimate" — honest, but it loses the person who wants a number before they'll pick up the phone. A page that explains *how* pricing works (square footage, stories, surface type, how bad the buildup is) captures that search and pre-qualifies the caller. |

**Gate:** Britton needs to confirm with Jase and Garrett what's actually sayable —
either real ranges, or at minimum the honest factors that drive a quote. **Do not
invent prices.** A page that explains the pricing *model* without naming dollar figures
still works and still ranks; a page with made-up numbers produces angry customers.

---

### 2. Pressure washing vs. soft washing: which does your house need?

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search intent** | Informational, high relevance — the searcher owns a dirty house |
| **Target page** | Expand the existing section on `/pressure-washing/`, or split to `/soft-washing/` |
| **Priority** | **High** |
| **Why it matters** | Genuinely useful, demonstrates expertise, and it's the distinction that justifies hiring a professional rather than renting a machine from the hardware store. It's already covered in a section and an FAQ on the pressure washing page. |

**Recommendation: expand in place first.** Only split it into its own page if Search
Console shows real impressions for soft-washing queries. Splitting too early means two
half-strength pages instead of one strong one.

## Priority 2 — Build once there's material

### 3. Ruston service area page

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search intent** | Local — "pressure washing Ruston LA" |
| **Target page** | New: `/service-area/ruston/` |
| **Priority** | **Medium** |
| **Why it matters** | Ruston is a genuinely different market — college town, heavy rental turnover, half an hour from Monroe. That difference is real content, not padding. |

**Gate — all three must be true:**
1. At least **3 before/after photos from actual Ruston jobs**
2. At least **1 Google review from a Ruston customer**
3. Something specific to say beyond "we also serve Ruston"

Without these it's a thin duplicate of `/service-area/` and does more harm than good.
See the reasoning in [SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md](SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md).

---

### 4. Before & after gallery

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search intent** | Trust / conversion, plus image search |
| **Target page** | New: `/gallery/` |
| **Priority** | **Medium** |
| **Why it matters** | Before/after photos are the most persuasive thing this business owns. There are currently 7 on the homepage. A dedicated gallery becomes worthwhile once there are enough to justify it, and it gives every future job photo somewhere to live. |

**Gate:** **15+ real before/after pairs.** Below that, the homepage gallery is enough
and a separate page just splits the content.

Every photo needs a caption naming the surface and the service. City names only where
the customer wouldn't mind — never a street address.

### 5. About Precision Wash & Landscape

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search intent** | Brand / trust (E-E-A-T) |
| **Target page** | New: `/about/` |
| **Priority** | **Medium** |
| **Why it matters** | Real owner information is a genuine trust and quality signal, and it matters for a trade where you're letting strangers onto your property. |

**Gate — Britton needs to get these facts from Jase and Garrett:**
- When and why they started the business
- How they know each other
- Anything about equipment, training, or prior experience
- Whether the business carries liability insurance *(if yes, this belongs on the site
  immediately — it's a real differentiator and a conversion lever, not just an About
  page item)*

**Do not build this page until those facts exist.** An About page made of adjectives is
worse than no About page. See the note in [SEO_AUDIT.md](SEO_AUDIT.md).

## Explicitly not recommended

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Weekly blog posts | Nobody reads them; they dilute the pages that matter. |
| "Top 10 pressure washing tips" | Generic, competes with national sites, attracts DIYers who won't hire you. |
| Pages for every nearby town | Doorway pages. Covered in the keyword map. |
| "Why choose us" page | This belongs woven into service pages, not isolated on a page nobody visits. |
| News/industry commentary | Zero customer value for a local service business. |
| AI-generated articles at volume | Directly contrary to how this site has been built, and a quality-signal risk. |

---

## Suggested sequencing

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Now** | Nothing. Get the domain live, the Profile created, and reviews coming in. Content is the *last* lever, not the first. |
| **Month 2–3** | Pressure washing cost page *(once pricing is confirmed)* |
| **Month 3–4** | Expand the soft-washing section in place |
| **Month 4–6** | About page *(once the facts exist)*; add insurance status site-wide if applicable |
| **Month 6+** | Check Search Console queries. **Rebuild this roadmap from that data.** |
| **When gated** | Ruston page (3 photos + 1 review); gallery (15+ pairs) |

The honest summary: this site does not need more pages right now. It needs to be
**reachable**, **verified**, and **reviewed**. Content is the third lever, and pulling
it first is the most common way small-business SEO effort gets wasted.
