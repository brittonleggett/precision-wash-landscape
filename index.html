Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home, or provide water and power?

Most jobs just need access to an outdoor water spigot and outlet — we bring our own equipment. You don't need to be home; just let us know how to access the areas being cleaned.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding, roof, or paint?

Yes. We use soft washing — lower pressure with the right cleaning solution — on delicate surfaces like siding, roofing, and painted areas, and save higher pressure for tougher surfaces like concrete and stone.

How often should I get my house or driveway washed?

Most homes in Louisiana's humidity benefit from a wash once or twice a year to stay ahead of mold, algae, and pollen buildup. We're happy to take a look and give you an honest recommendation.

What kind of junk do you haul away?

Tree limbs, old furniture, yard debris, and general household junk. If you're not sure whether we can take it, just call or text and describe what you've got.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we give free, no-obligation estimates before any work starts. Call or text Jase or Garrett with a few photos or details and we'll get you a number fast.
